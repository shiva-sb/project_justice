About Dr.Justice
S. Vimala

ustice S Vimala of the Madras high court retired from service on Thursday. With her superannuation, the total number of judges in the high court has come down to 60 as against the sanctioned strengthof 75. Justice Vimala was the first woman to be appointed as the judge of Chennai Mahila Court in 2002, first woman appointed as director of a state judicial academy in 2006. In 2011, she was elevated as additional judge of the high court and subsequently appointed as a permanent judge on October 21, 2013.
